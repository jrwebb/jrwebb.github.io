About

Building products
that earn loyalty

I've spent over 15 years inside some of the world's most respected media organisations leading product teams that work at the intersection of editorial ambition and subscription economics. The common thread: I help organisations turn readers into subscribers, and subscribers into advocates.

I focus mostly on retention as it's where I believe the best companies separate themselves. Working across both feature and product level, I help teams build the habits that keep users active and extend lifetime value.

As Group Product Manager at the Financial Times, my teams scaled myFT from 2% to 73% subscriber adoption and later formed the community pillar that connected live events with digital subscriptions. Both moved revenue. Both required working across the grain of large organisations to get stuff done.

I'm currently exploring senior product management roles, as well as select consulting and contract engagements where I can make a meaningful difference.
